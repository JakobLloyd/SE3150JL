Elevator technical specifications

The building has exactly three floors numbered 1 through 3. The elevator starts at floor 1, idle, with closed doors.
The elevator reports its current floor, motion direction, door state, load, and emergency state.
The elevator accepts a destination only for a valid floor.
The elevator ignores a destination equal to its current floor.
The elevator moves one floor per step toward its destination.
The elevator stops when it reaches its destination.
The elevator reports Up while moving to a higher floor.
The elevator reports Down while moving to a lower floor.
The elevator reports Idle while stopped.
The elevator opens its doors only while idle.
The elevator closes its doors before moving.
The elevator will not move while its doors are open.
The elevator opens its doors when it reaches a destination.
The elevator can queue multiple valid destinations.
The elevator serves queued destinations in request order.
The elevator ignores duplicate destinations already queued.
The elevator removes a destination after arriving there.
The elevator rejects a destination outside floors 1 through 3.
The elevator accepts a passenger only when doors are open.
The elevator rejects a passenger when doors are closed.
The elevator tracks the number of passengers.
The elevator rejects boarding when capacity would be exceeded.
The elevator rejects a negative passenger count.
The elevator allows passengers to leave only when doors are open.
The elevator cannot move when overloaded.
The elevator enters emergency mode when its emergency button is pressed.
Emergency mode stops the elevator immediately.
Emergency mode opens the doors when the elevator is idle.
The reset button clears emergency mode while the elevator is stopped.
The reset button does not clear emergency mode while the elevator is moving.
The elevator refuses new destinations during emergency mode.
The elevator can report whether it is full.
The elevator can report whether it is idle.
