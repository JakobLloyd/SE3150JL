Notes for Issue Reporting

Well-formed "characterized" bug reports:

Have a good title
Clearly outline the problem
Include detailed steps to re-create the problem
Include backup information to support your position (do your homework)
Indicate clearly
Describe consequences that the user experiences (who is affected?)

You may include pictures, screenshots, diagrams, code-fragments, proposed solutions (add artifacts on GitHub after post)


example issue report:

**Title**
state the outline of the problem.
**Steps**
Observe the response, paste the output.
**Backup Info**
The specifications and issues in simple steps
**Severity**
1-5 on importance 1 is immediately 5 is some other time.
**Consequences**
List the consequences and repercussions of the issues.
